## What it is

Hydration here means the total amount of water and other drinks you had today, in millilitres. You log it yourself.

## Why it matters in space

The fluid shift toward the head can dull the feeling of thirst, so it is easy to drink less than you need without noticing. Water is also a limited, recycled supply.

Too little water can leave you tired, dizzy or with a faster heart rate. Over a long mission it also makes the urine more concentrated, which, together with the extra calcium released from bone, raises the chance of kidney stones.

## When to worry

Thirst is not a reliable guide in space. Go by what you logged.

Take it seriously when the app shows red, or when low intake comes with dizziness, tiredness or a raised heart rate. Follow the actions on the result screen and check again later in the day.
