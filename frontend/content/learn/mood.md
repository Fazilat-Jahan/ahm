## What it is

Mood is your own rating of how you feel today, from very low to great. There is no right answer. An honest rating is more useful than a good one.

## Why it matters in space

A long mission means the same few people, the same small space, and a long way from family and from Earth. That can slowly wear down mood and motivation, often without one clear cause.

A daily rating makes a slow slide visible, to you first, so something can be done early. It is tracked together with sleep and stress because the three affect each other.

## When to worry

Low days happen to everyone. Look at the trend, not a single day.

Take it seriously when the app shows red, when low mood lasts for days, or when it comes with poor sleep or anxiety. Talk to a crewmate you trust. If you ever have thoughts of harming yourself, say so in the check-in: the app will tell you to reach the crew and the flight surgeon straight away.
