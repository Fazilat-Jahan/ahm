## What it is

Body temperature is how warm the inside of your body is. It stays within a narrow range when you are well and rises when the body is fighting an infection.

## Why it matters in space

Spaceflight can change how the immune system responds, and a sealed habitat means the whole crew shares the same air and the same microbes. An infection that would be minor on Earth is harder to deal with far from help.

The habitat also has to manage its own heat, so a warm cabin or hard exercise can raise your reading for a while. A daily temperature gives an early warning of illness.

## When to worry

Measure the same way and at a similar time each day so readings can be compared. If you have just exercised or the cabin is warm, rest and measure again.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or critical, or when a raised temperature comes with chills, a cough, a rash or feeling unwell. Tell the crew early: others may be affected too.
