## What it is

Exercise minutes are the time you spent today on resistance exercise (working against a load) and on cardio exercise (raising your heart rate). The app adds the two together.

## Why it matters in space

On Earth, bones and muscles are loaded all day just by standing and walking. In microgravity, and even in the weaker gravity of the Moon or Mars, most of that load disappears, and the body starts to lose bone and muscle it no longer seems to need.

This app cannot measure bone density. Exercise is tracked instead, because it is the main protection the crew has. Resistance work matters most for bone, cardio for the heart and lungs.

## When to worry

A missed session now and then is not a problem. Several low days in a row are, because the loss builds up quietly.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or an exercise trend alert, or when back, joint or muscle pain stops you from exercising. Log the pain as a symptom so it is not missed.
