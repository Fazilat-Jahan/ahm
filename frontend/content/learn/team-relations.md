## What it is

Team relations is your own rating of how well the crew is getting along today. It is optional, and it is about the group, not about any one person.

## Why it matters in space

A crew that lives and works in a small space for months cannot step away from a disagreement. Small frictions can build up, and tension makes shared work slower and mistakes more likely.

Rating it regularly shows when tension is growing, early enough to talk about it calmly.

## When to worry

A tense day after a hard task is normal. A low rating that keeps coming back is the signal.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or a team trend alert. Talk to a crewmate or the commander, and mention it to the flight surgeon or psychologist if it continues.
