## What it is

Blood pressure is the force of blood against the walls of your arteries. It is written as two numbers: the higher one (systolic) when the heart squeezes, and the lower one (diastolic) when it relaxes between beats.

## Why it matters in space

In microgravity, fluid that normally sits in the legs moves up toward the chest and head. The body responds by adjusting how much fluid it keeps and how the blood vessels behave, and blood pressure can shift as a result.

The heart and vessels also get less of a daily workout than on Earth. Regular readings show whether your circulation is settling into a steady pattern or drifting.

## When to worry

Blood pressure moves with activity, stress and even talking. If a reading looks off, sit quietly for a few minutes and measure again. The app keeps both readings.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or critical, when readings stay raised for several days, or when a high reading comes with chest pain or a strong headache. The result screen tells you who to contact.
