## What it is

Your resting heart rate is how many times your heart beats in one minute while you are sitting still and calm. It is one of the simplest signs of how hard your heart and circulation are working.

## Why it matters in space

Without the usual pull of gravity, the heart does not have to push blood "uphill", and body fluid moves toward the chest and head. Over weeks the heart and blood vessels adapt to this lighter workload, which can change your usual resting rate.

A resting rate that is higher than your normal can also be an early sign of stress, poor sleep, too little water, or an illness starting. Watching it every day makes a slow change easy to spot.

## When to worry

One odd reading is often just a measurement taken too soon after moving. Rest and measure again before reading much into it.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or critical, when the rate stays outside your usual range for several days, or when it comes with chest pain, palpitations or dizziness. The result screen tells you who to contact.
