## What it is

Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) is the share of your red blood cells that are carrying oxygen. A small clip on a fingertip, called a pulse oximeter, reads it in a few seconds.

## Why it matters in space

Every breath you take comes from the life-support system of the habitat or the suit. If the oxygen level drops, or carbon dioxide builds up in a corner with poor air flow, your body can be short of oxygen before you clearly notice it.

SpO2 is a quick way to check that the air you are breathing is doing its job.

## When to worry

Cold fingers, movement or a loose clip can give a falsely low number. Warm your hand, keep still and measure again.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or critical, or when a lower reading comes with breathlessness, a headache, dizziness or trouble concentrating. Sit down, tell the crew, and follow the result screen. Others may be breathing the same air.
