## What it is

Radiation dose is the amount of radiation your body has absorbed, read from a personal dosimeter if you have one. The app adds up your daily readings and compares the total with the mission budget.

## Why it matters in space

The atmosphere and magnetic field of Earth block most radiation from the Sun and from deep space. A crew beyond them is exposed all the time, with short, stronger bursts during solar storms.

Radiation damage builds up over the whole mission and cannot be felt day to day. Keeping a running total is the only way to know how much of the budget has been used and when to take extra shelter.

## When to worry

This app tracks exposure only. It cannot tell you anything about long-term health effects.

Take it seriously when the app shows yellow or red against the budget, or when a storm warning is given. Follow the mission shelter procedure and contact the flight surgeon as the result screen says.
