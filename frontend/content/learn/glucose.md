## What it is

Fasting blood glucose is the amount of sugar in your blood after several hours without food, usually first thing after waking. It shows how well your body is managing its main fuel.

## Why it matters in space

The body can handle sugar differently during spaceflight, and less physical loading, disturbed sleep and stress all play a part. Food on a long mission is also limited in variety and supply, so eating enough of the right things takes attention.

A fasting reading is a simple daily check that nutrition and energy balance are staying on track.

## When to worry

Make sure the reading really was fasting: a snack or sweet drink beforehand changes it. If in doubt, note it and measure again the next morning.

Take it seriously when the app shows red or critical, or when a low reading comes with dizziness or unusual tiredness. The result screen tells you what to do and who to contact.
