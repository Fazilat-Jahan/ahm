## What it is

Sleep duration is how many hours you actually slept, not how long you were in your sleep station. The app also asks you to rate how good the sleep felt.

## Why it matters in space

There is no natural day and night on a mission. Light, noise, workload and shifting schedules can all push your body clock out of step, and sleeping while floating or in a small bunk takes getting used to.

Short or broken sleep affects mood, attention and reaction time, which matters when the crew has to rely on itself. Sleep is also closely tied to how isolation feels.

## When to worry

One short night is normal. What counts is the pattern over several days, which the Trends screen shows.

Take it seriously when the app shows red, when several short nights come in a row, or when poor sleep comes with low mood. Follow the actions on the result screen and tell someone on the crew.
